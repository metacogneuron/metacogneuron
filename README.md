# Hi there 👋, I'm Samuel Gali

I am an AI Engineer & Researcher as well as a Computer Science Senior at the University of Lagos. Sitting at the intersection of research and applied AI engineering, my work focuses on contributing to the progress of the African NLP space and building Agentic AI systems that go beyond static text processing to reasoning, planning, and autonomous execution.

---

### **🧐 About Me**

- **🔭 I’m currently working on:** Building autonomous agentic workflows that bridge the gap between LLMs and actionable API execution.
- **🌱 I’m currently learning:** The intricacies of **LangGraph** to move from linear chains to cyclic, state-aware agent architectures.
- **✍️ Philosophy:** I treat writing as a debugging tool for my thoughts. When I'm not training models or dissecting code, I write about the philosophy of AI and my technical journey on my **[Medium Blog](https://medium.com/@lazy-monster)**.
